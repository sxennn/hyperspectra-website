FRAMESYNC · QUICK START

From blank canvas
to ready-to-post.

Make one composition, then download it in Feed and Story sizes.

  1. 01
    Choose a layout

    Pick a template on the left. Gallery is for one photo; Film strip uses up to three; Midnight star film has four frames; Scrapbook layers two photos.

  2. 02
    Add your photos

    Choose Try sample photos, drop files into Your photos, or browse for JPG, PNG, or WEBP images. Photos are processed on your device.

  3. 03
    Fill the frames

    Click a photo to fill the highlighted frame. Click a different frame first to choose where the next photo goes; click a filled frame to replace it. Photos stay uncropped.

  4. 04
    Style both formats

    Use the Design Kit to adjust the background, type, caption, borders, and spacing. These choices stay in sync across Feed and Story.

    For a teaser, choose Story teaser and select a mode. For a wide photo, choose Panorama.

  5. 05
    Preview and download

    Use Paired view, Feed only, or Story only to check your design. Download PNGs at 1080 × 1350 for Feed and 1080 × 1920 for Story. Panorama also creates two separate Feed slides.

Tip: your work stays in this browser session.
